Safeguarding software can make accountability and controlled record-keeping easier. It must never delay a response to an immediate concern or substitute for the church's reporting procedures, professional advice and safeguarding judgement.

## Questions to ask

- Which roles can open, change, export or audit sensitive records, and is that permission model usable in practice?
- Can the church record the information it needs without treating the tool as a legal or safeguarding decision-maker?
- What happens to historic records when the safeguarding officer changes or the supplier contract ends?
- Have the safeguarding lead and relevant senior leaders tested the workflow against current local policy?
