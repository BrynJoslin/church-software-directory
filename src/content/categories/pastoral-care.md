Pastoral-care tools can help a team remember follow-up and share limited operational information. The church remains responsible for its boundaries, confidentiality, safeguarding response and appropriate referral routes.

## Questions to ask

- What information is necessary to coordinate care, and what should not be stored in the system at all?
- Can sensitive notes be restricted to the right people and reviewed when pastoral roles change?
- How are safeguarding concerns separated from routine pastoral contact and escalated without delay?
- Does the system support a respectful handover without creating an uncontrolled archive of personal history?
