A rota tool can reduce coordination work, but it cannot make an unclear team structure fair or sustainable. Agree the roles, rest expectations and owner for last-minute changes before importing everyone’s contact details.

## Questions to ask

- Can a volunteer state unavailability, request cover and see only the information they need?
- Does the real Sunday, weekday or event workflow fit the church's teams and service pattern?
- Who approves a swap or role change, and how are urgent gaps communicated?
- Can the church leave with usable rota and contact information if it changes tool or coordinator?
