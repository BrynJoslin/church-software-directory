Booking software can prevent double-booking and reduce routine administration, but it does not settle a church's letting policy or decide whether a particular booking is appropriate. Map the whole journey from enquiry to invoice, access and cancellation before committing.

## Questions to ask

- Can staff see every room, recurring booking, provisional hold and cancellation in one reliable calendar?
- How are deposits, invoices, refunds and overdue payments handled, and what information must the treasurer receive?
- Can public users book only the spaces, prices and times the church intends to expose?
- Who owns hirer records, terms, permissions and handover when a buildings volunteer changes?
