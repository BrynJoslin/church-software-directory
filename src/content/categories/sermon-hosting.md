Sermon hosting is only helpful if it makes regular publishing sustainable. Decide who owns the recording, checks it before release and responds when a sermon needs correcting or removing.

## Questions to ask

- Can volunteers publish a recording reliably after an ordinary service?
- Is the archive easy to search, embed and move if the service changes?
- What permissions are needed for speakers, interviews, music and livestreams?
- Does the church need public reach, private access or both?
