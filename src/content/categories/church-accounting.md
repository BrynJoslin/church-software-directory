Accounting software can help a church keep records, track funds set aside for a
particular purpose and prepare reports. It should strengthen existing financial
controls, not replace them with assumptions.

## Questions to ask

- Can it keep money limited to a stated purpose separate from money the church can use more freely?
- How will the church match bank payments to its records and check the result?
- Can the treasurer, trustees and accountant get the reports and access they need?
- What data can the church export if it changes provider?
