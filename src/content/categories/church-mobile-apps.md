A church app is only useful if it gives people a clearer, more convenient route than the church website, email or existing management platform. Decide who will keep its content current and who owns the app-store accounts before commissioning one.

## Questions to ask

- Which specific member journeys need an app rather than a responsive website?
- Is the church, not a supplier or volunteer, the owner of its Apple and Google developer accounts?
- What content, notifications and personal data will be managed in the app?
- Can the church export content and move to another service if support or strategy changes?
