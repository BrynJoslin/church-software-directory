A church website succeeds when people can quickly find accurate service information, contact details and next steps. A template or visual refresh cannot compensate for out-of-date content or unclear ownership.

## Questions to ask

- Who will update the site every week, and what training will they need?
- Is the domain name registered to the church and recoverable if a volunteer leaves?
- How will forms, consent and safeguarding contact routes be managed?
- What is included in the subscription, and what needs separate design or technical support?
