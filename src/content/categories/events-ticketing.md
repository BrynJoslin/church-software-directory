An event tool can remove repetitive registration work, but it also makes its pricing and payment journey visible to guests. Test the complete experience from booking to cancellation before committing.

## Questions to ask

- Is the checkout experience clear about the ticket price, booking fees and refund policy?
- Can the team handle free events, paid events, groups, courses and waitlists without manual workarounds?
- Does the platform send attendee money directly to the church’s chosen payment provider?
- Can staff export an attendance list without giving every volunteer broad access to personal data?
