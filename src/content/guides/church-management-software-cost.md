Church management software can cost nothing in licence fees or hundreds each month. The public subscription is rarely the full cost. The useful budget is a three-year operating budget that includes the work and services needed to make the system dependable.

## Published examples, not a price league table

| Product | Published entry point | What changes it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [ChurchSuite](/software/churchsuite/) | £9/month for 0–99 contacts and one module; £32.50/month for six or more modules. | Active contacts, modules and storage. VAT included where applicable. |
| [iKnow Church](/software/iknow-church/) | £22/month for fewer than 50 adult members. | Member band and separate finance charges; VAT included in published pricing. |
| [ChurchDesk](/software/churchdesk/) | £54/month Basic package. | Annual billing, contact/user limits and VAT excluded. |
| [ChurchTools](/software/churchtools/) | €29/month for up to 100 people. | People, campuses, resources and VAT. |
| [Elvanto](/software/elvanto/) | $19/month for up to 50 active adults. | Active adults, annual billing and a stated setup cost. |
| [ChMeetings](/software/chmeetings/) | Free to 50 people; paid plans from $12/month for 100 people. | People count, add-ons and US-dollar billing. |

See [ChurchSuite pricing](https://churchsuite.com/pricing/), [iKnow Church pricing](https://www.iknowchurch.co.uk/iknow), [ChurchDesk pricing](https://churchdesk.com/en/pricing/), [ChurchTools pricing](https://church.tools/en/pricing/) and [Elvanto pricing](https://www.elvanto.com/pricing/). Prices and tax treatment change; check the live supplier page and written proposal.

## Budget these seven lines

1. Subscription: modules, people/contact bands, billing frequency and renewal.
2. Tax and currency: VAT inclusion, exchange-rate exposure and payment method.
3. Migration: cleaning duplicates, importing records and checking results.
4. Training and setup: configuration, on-site help, documentation and volunteer time.
5. Connected services: messages, payments, giving, labels, devices and integrations.
6. Ongoing administration: access reviews, data quality, reporting and support.
7. Exit: export, cancellation terms, archive and replacement migration.

The hidden cost is the unmanaged process left outside the product. A lower subscription can cost more if it leaves leaders manually reconciling lists every week. Conversely, a full suite wastes money if it replaces simple, stable work with configuration nobody can maintain.

## Ask for a comparable quote

Give every supplier the same scenario: active people, administrators, modules, sites, rooms, check-ins, messages, giving volume and implementation support. Ask for first-year and renewal totals, with VAT and every known fee stated. Record unknowns as “pricing needs verification”, not zero.
