Gift Aid support is not a badge. It is a chain of work: a valid declaration, an eligible donation, an accurate donor record, a claim or report, correction handling and finance reconciliation. A platform can accept donations without supporting that whole chain.

## Current evidence-led shortlist

| Product | What current supplier evidence supports | First question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [ChurchSuite](/software/churchsuite/) | UK Giving documentation covers declarations, Gift Aid Small Donation Scheme and producing/submitting claims to HMRC. | Which Giving plan, payment fees and finance process apply to us? |
| [iKnow Church](/software/iknow-church/) | Supplier documents Gift Aid and publishes separate finance charges. | How are claims, corrections and charges handled in our workflow? |
| [KindLink](/software/kindlink/) | Free plan includes UK Gift Aid records; paid plans describe automated submission. | Which data, payment and HMRC roles remain with the church? |
| [Stewardship](/software/stewardship/) | UK Partner Account and Gift Aid service; published fee examples deduct from gifts and applicable Gift Aid. | How do deductions and reporting reconcile to our accounts? |
| [Give A Little](/software/give-a-little/) | Supplier describes Gift Aid in its UK contactless and online giving context. | Which declaration and claim steps are included for our arrangement? |
| [Parish Giving Scheme](/software/parish-giving-scheme/) | Regular Direct Debit and Gift Aid focus for participating Church of England dioceses. | Is our parish/diocese eligible and how will it reconcile locally? |

This is not a complete market list. Gift Aid is not confirmed in the current records for several broader platforms, including Planning Center, ChurchDesk and ChMeetings. That means ask; it does not mean “no”.

## Test the whole route

Ask the supplier to demonstrate a fictional donor from declaration to claim or report. Include an ineligible gift, a changed declaration, an incorrect amount and a cancelled payment. Then answer:

1. Where is the declaration stored, and who can alter it?
2. Which donations are marked eligible, and why?
3. Does the system prepare, submit or only export the claim data?
4. How are exceptions, overclaims and corrections recorded?
5. What fees apply to transactions, claims or account services?
6. How does the finance team reconcile the amount received, fees and Gift Aid?

ChurchSuite’s published guide is unusually specific: it states that UK customers can manage declarations, the Small Donations Scheme and submit claims. [ChurchSuite Giving](https://support.churchsuite.com/article/455-getting-started-with-the-giving-module). KindLink’s published pricing distinguishes Gift Aid records from automated submission. [KindLink pricing](https://www.kindlink.org/pricing). Those distinctions matter.

## Do not let Gift Aid create a duplicate database

A specialist giving platform can be the correct choice. Decide explicitly where the authoritative donor, declaration and finance records live, how changes travel and who resolves mismatches. A donation form, a bank statement and a people database must not become three unowned versions of the same record.

Gift Aid administration does not remove the church’s responsibility for financial controls or data handling. Restrict access to donor information, keep evidence, establish review steps and seek appropriate professional advice when the church’s circumstances require it.
