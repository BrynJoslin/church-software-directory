## Outreach principles

- Factual listings and corrections remain free.
- Payment must not affect editorial conclusions or verification status.
- Do not imply endorsement merely because a provider is listed.
- Clearly label supplier-provided information.
- Use actual traffic and click data rather than vague exposure claims.
- Avoid bespoke arrangements that create significant ongoing work.
