# Guide and keyword backlog

This is the working queue for potential guides and the search terms they are
intended to answer. It is a planning record, not a publishing schedule or a
source of factual product claims.

Add a row when a query represents a distinct church decision. Do not create a
new page merely because the wording differs: record close variants against the
same canonical guide, or route them to an existing page. Every new guide must
still meet the research, evidence and editorial requirements in
[`GUIDE_STANDARD.md`](GUIDE_STANDARD.md).

## How to prioritise

Use the priority score to decide which *brief* to prepare next. It is a
transparent triage tool, not a traffic forecast or a reason to publish before
the evidence is ready.

| Factor | 1 point | 2 points | 3 points |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Demand | Fewer than 20 supplied monthly searches | 20–49 supplied monthly searches | 50 or more supplied monthly searches |
| Decision value | Mainly informational or broad navigation | A meaningful operational choice | A high-consequence purchase, migration or governance decision |
| Evidence readiness | No relevant category or too little source-backed data | Relevant category exists, but material evidence gaps are likely | Relevant category and several source-backed listings already exist |

Add the three figures for a score out of nine. Work the highest-scoring,
distinct `Ready to brief` item first. If research cannot support a useful,
independent guide, lower its readiness score and record the blocking evidence
gap rather than drafting a thin page.

Suggested cadence:

1. When a keyword is found, add it with its source, date and the query's
   apparent decision.
2. Group variants under one canonical target before assigning a score.
3. Prepare a one-paragraph brief and evidence plan for the first item only.
4. Move it through `Briefing`, `Researching`, `Drafting`, `Review`,
   `Published` or `Parked`, recording why a material item is parked.
5. Once search data is available, replace supplied volume with dated
   Search Console evidence and review the order monthly.

### External decision signals

The following are research-planning sources, checked on 27 July 2026. They
establish that the decisions and responsibilities exist; they are not evidence
for individual product claims.

- The Church of England publishes current guidance on
  [records and information management](https://www.churchofengland.org/about/libraries-and-archives/records-and-information-management),
  including a record-retention schedule updated in May 2026, and a separate
  resource on
  [managing church hall bookings](https://www.churchofengland.org/resources/resourcing-church-administration/buildings-administration/managing-church-hall-bookings).
- The Charity Commission places continuing responsibility on trustees through
  its guidance on
  [safeguarding for charities](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/safeguarding-for-charities-and-trustees)
  and
  [internal financial controls](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/internal-financial-controls-for-charities-cc8/internal-financial-controls-for-charities).
- The ICO explains that religious belief and health information can be
  [special category data](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/special-category-data/what-is-special-category-data/)
  and that processor contracts must address
  [return or deletion of data at contract end](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/accountability-and-governance/contracts-and-liabilities-between-controllers-and-processors-multi/what-needs-to-be-included-in-the-contract/).
- Current denominational material connects church streaming with consent,
  safeguarding, copyright and accessibility. See the Methodist Church's
  [recording consent and accessibility guide](https://www.methodist.org.uk/for-churches/digital-church/online-worship-toolkit/recording-consent-and-accessibility-guide/)
  and the Church of Scotland's
  [livestreaming guidance](https://www.churchofscotland.org.uk/about-us/departments/communications-department/communication-resources/live-streaming-guidance).
- Charity-sector guidance identifies duplicated spreadsheets, unclear ownership
  and poor adoption as signals for a database review. See Charity Digital's
  [CRM questions and migration discussion](https://charitydigital.org.uk/topics/answering-your-questions-about-choosing-the-right-crm-8686).
- Search results for safeguarding and venue-booking comparisons are currently
  led in part by supplier-authored guides, including
  [Plinth's safeguarding guide](https://www.plinth.org.uk/complete-guide/safeguarding-software-charities)
  and
  [Softbooker's venue guide](https://softbooker.app/blog/venue-booking-software-guide-2025/).
  They indicate an active decision format but cannot establish neutral product
  facts. An independent church-specific guide should add clearer scope,
  evidence provenance and limits rather than imitate their rankings.

## Maintenance rules

- Keep one row per decision, with keyword variants in the same row.
- Preserve the supplied source and date for every volume figure; do not present
  a planning figure as measured site performance.
- Prefer Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools and site-search evidence once
  those are configured, while recognising that low-volume data is not proof of
  no demand.
- Record a guide as `Published` only after it satisfies the full guide standard
  and the required checks; a draft or a generated file is not publication.
- When a guide is published, retain its row with the canonical URL and change
  the next action to the next evidence or performance review.
